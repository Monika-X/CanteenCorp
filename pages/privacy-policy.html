1. Categories of Data We Collect

To provide personalized dining, tax-exempt voucher processing, and dietary safety, CanteenCorp processes the following categories of data:

  • Identity & Corporate Credentials: Employee ID, full name, corporate email address, and assigned department or floor wing.
  • Cafeteria POS & Order Telemetry: Meal items selected, timestamp of transaction, terminal ID, and dietary modifications chosen.
  • Dietary & Allergen Declarations: Voluntary employee preferences (e.g. Jain, nut allergy, gluten sensitivity, diabetic indicators) strictly used for kitchen cross-contamination warnings.
  • Meal Wallet Financials: Balance top-up history, corporate subsidy allocation, and invoice receipts required under Indian Income Tax Act perquisite allowances.

2. Biometric, RFID & NFC Hardware Protocols

Where corporate clients utilize NFC employee ID cards or biometric turnstile authentication at cafeteria entrances:

Tokenization Only: No raw biometric fingerprints or facial scans are stored on CanteenCorp POS servers. Our readers receive cryptographic hash tokens directly verified against client enterprise Active Directory/HRMS systems.

Offline Vaulting: POS terminals operate on air-gapped local subnetworks with TLS 1.3 encrypted sync to our central cloud database every 15 minutes.

3. Meal Wallet Financial Transaction Logs

All prepaid wallet balances, top-ups, and corporate meal allowances comply with RBI Prepaid Payment Instrument (PPI) directives and ISO 27001 data isolation policies. Audited quarterly by independent CERT-In empanelled auditors.
