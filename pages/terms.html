Master Terms of Corporate Service

These terms govern all corporate dining facility contracts, automated meal subscription programs, executive catering bookings, and wallet APIs.

Standard Enterprise MSA v4.2 Effective: July 1, 2026 Jurisdiction: Mumbai, India

2. Prepaid Meal Wallets & Corporate Subscriptions

Tax-Exempt Perquisite Usage: Meal wallet balances issued under employer-subsidized plans comply with Rule 3(7)(iii) of the Indian Income Tax Rules, 1962 (tax exemption up to ₹50 per meal). Balances are non-transferable to third-party bank accounts.

Balance Expiry & Rollover: Active employee balances roll over month-to-month during active tenure. Upon resignation or corporate off-boarding, unspent personal top-up balances are refunded to the employee's original payment method within 14 business days.

3. Food Safety, Hygiene & Allergen Disclaimers

FSSAI & ISO Compliance: CanteenCorp warrants that all meal preparation adheres to FSSAI Schedule 4 hygiene norms and ISO 22000:2018 food safety standards.

Allergen Notice: While CanteenCorp maintains strict allergen segregation protocols, our kitchens process nuts, dairy, wheat, gluten, and soy. Employees with severe airborne anaphylactic sensitivities must register with the on-site culinary manager.

4. Enterprise Billing & Headcount Minimums

Minimum Guaranteed Headcount (MGH): Contracts are structured on agreed minimum daily headcount tiers. If daily actual cafeteria attendance falls below 85% of MGH due to hybrid remote policies, billing is adjusted per contract addendum schedules.

Payment Terms: Monthly consolidated invoices are issued on the 1st of each calendar month with Net-30 payment terms via RTGS/NEFT.

5. Term, Cancellation & Dispute Arbitration

Standard enterprise agreements carry an initial 12-month term, renewable annually. Either party may terminate with 60 days written notice. All disputes are subject to the exclusive jurisdiction of courts in Mumbai, India, under the Arbitration and Conciliation Act, 1996.
