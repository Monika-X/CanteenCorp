Our Journey

14 Years of
Culinary Innovation

2012

Founded in Mumbai

CanteenCorp started with a single 200-person tech company in Powai, with a small team of 8 and a vision for better corporate food.

2015

First 10 Enterprise Clients

Rapid growth led us to open our central kitchen facility in BKC and onboard our first enterprise clients including banking and IT firms.

2018

Launched Admin Dashboard

Released our proprietary canteen management platform — analytics, meal wallets, and real-time reporting for HR teams.

2021

ISO 22000 Certification

Achieved food safety management certification across all 12 kitchen facilities, setting new industry standards.

2024

Expanded Pan-India

Operations now span Mumbai, Bengaluru, Hyderabad, Pune, and Chennai — serving 2,400+ meals per day.

2026

47+ Enterprise Partners

Today, CanteenCorp is India's most trusted corporate dining brand with 320+ team members and 98.6% satisfaction.

What We Stand For

Our Culture & Core Values

The principles that guide every meal we cook, every team we hire, and every client we serve.

🍽️

Food is Respect

Every plate we serve is an act of care. We believe that well-nourished employees are more creative, collaborative, and fulfilled — and we take that responsibility seriously.

🌍

Planet-Conscious

Zero single-use plastic. Composting programs. Local sourcing. We're on a path to carbon neutrality by 2028.

🤝

Partner First

We don't see our clients as accounts — we're true hospitality partners, invested in their employees' happiness.

📈

Continuous Improvement

Monthly recipe refreshes, quarterly menu surveys, and annual nutrition audits — we never stop improving the experience. Last year we made 214 menu improvements based on employee feedback.

🧑‍🤝‍🧑

Inclusive Dining

Jain, vegan, gluten-free, diabetic-friendly — every dietary need is met with equal care and creativity.

🔬

Science-Backed Nutrition

Every menu is co-designed with our in-house nutritionists to meet optimal macro and micronutrient targets.
